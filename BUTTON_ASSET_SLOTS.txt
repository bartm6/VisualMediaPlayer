BUTTON ASSET SLOTS
==================

Portable build:
  VisualMediaPlayer-release-1.0\Portable\ButtonAssets\

Installer source:
  VisualMediaPlayer-release-1.0\Installer\ButtonAssets\

The app keeps the built-in visuals until you add a replacement file.
Drop a PNG or SVG into ButtonAssets using the slot name, then restart the app.
Example: Play.png, Back.svg, Background_Inactive.svg.

See ButtonAssets\README.txt for every slot name.
